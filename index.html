// Areas of expertise

Three disciplines.
One mandate.

We meet you wherever you are on the curve — from a board-level "should we tokenize?" memo, to mainnet issuance, to ongoing lifecycle operations.

01 / Strategy—

Strategy & structuring

From thesis to investment committee. We translate your asset class, jurisdiction, and distribution model into a tokenization roadmap that holds up under legal and commercial scrutiny.

  • Market entry & opportunity sizing
  • Token economic & structural design
  • Regulatory positioning (EU, CH, SG, AE)
  • Vendor & chain selection
02 / Execution—

Execution & engineering

A senior delivery team with audited smart-contract patterns, custody integrations, and operational runbooks. We build, you ship — without owning the engineering risk in-house.

  • Smart-contract architecture & audits
  • Custody, KYT & transfer agent integration
  • Distribution rails & investor onboarding
  • Off-chain ⇄ on-chain reconciliation
03 / Issuance—

Issuance & lifecycle

Primary issuance, secondary liquidity, corporate actions, and reporting — orchestrated as a single operating system rather than ten disconnected vendors.

  • Primary & secondary issuance ops
  • Coupon, redemption & corporate actions
  • Investor reporting & NAV pipelines
  • Liquidity venue & market-maker setup

// How we work

Two phases.
Senior bench throughout.

Most engagements run 4–9 months from kickoff to first investor allocation. No throwaway slides — every deliverable feeds the build.

Phase 01 · 4–8 weeks

Discovery & Strategy

A diagnostic sprint that converts intent into an executable, board-ready plan.
We pressure-test the commercial thesis, the regulatory path, and the operating model before a single line of code is written. You leave with conviction — or a clear "not yet."
  • A.Diagnostic — current state, gaps, stakeholder map.
  • B.Market mapping — comparable issuances, distribution channels, pricing.
  • C.Token design — structure, rights, lifecycle events, transfer rules.
  • D.Regulatory — jurisdictional fit, license inventory, counsel coordination.
Deliverable Investment-committee memo · token term sheet · execution roadmap with vendor & budget envelope.
Phase 02 · 12–32 weeks

Execution & Launch

A senior delivery squad embedded with your team until the first investor settles on-chain.
We run engineering, integrations, legal handoffs, and go-to-market in parallel — with weekly steering, transparent burn, and clear off-ramps at every gate.
  • A.Build — smart contracts, audits, infra, observability.
  • B.Integration — custody, KYT, transfer agent, banking rails.
  • C.Issuance — primary launch, allocation, settlement, reporting.
  • D.Lifecycle — corporate actions, secondary venues, ongoing ops.
Deliverable Live tokenized product on mainnet · audited contracts · investor portal · operational runbook handed to your team.
